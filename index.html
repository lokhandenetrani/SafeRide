    The Reality On Indian Roads

    The Safety Gap

    A conventional helmet protects your head during an impact. However, it cannot verify whether you actually fastened the chin strap, nor can it call for medical help when you are incapacitated. SafeRide was designed to close both vital gaps.

    Primary Roadblocks Identified in Student Commuter Field Research

    01 / HABIT

    Improper Strap Use

    Riders often wear helmets loose without latching the strap, causing the helmet to fling off during collision.

    02 / ISOLATION

    Zero Crash Awareness

    Standard helmets are passive plastic shells unable to detect sudden high-G deceleration or rider rollover.

    03 / DELAY

    Emergency Response Lag

    The "Golden Hour" after a crash is lost if an injured rider cannot reach their phone or is alone on rural roads.

    04 / BARRIER

    Cost Prohibitive Smart Helmets

    Imported smart helmets cost ₹8,000–₹25,000, putting life-saving IoT out of reach for students and delivery riders.

    05 / USABILITY

    Weight & Rigid Foam

    Modifying helmet foam damages safety certification. A lightweight external add-on preserves certified structural EPS.

    The SafeRide Solution

    One Small Module. Two Critical Safety Checks.

    SafeRide attaches non-invasively to your existing helmet's chin strap and shell, adding real-time buckle verification and crash-triggered emergency SOS alerts.

    SAFETY CHECK 1

    Strap-Check Verification

    A tactile or conductive micro-limit interlock embedded in the chin strap harness continuously verifies whether the buckle is securely fastened before and throughout the ride.

    • Prevents dangerous unbuckled riding that causes helmet ejection during an accident.
    • Gentle audible reminder buzzer nudges the rider if moving with strap open.
    • Low-power pull-up sensor design consumes micro-amps of standby current.
    Hardware: Tactile Interlock / Conductive Buckle Clasp · Debounced via ESP32 GPIO
    SAFETY CHECK 2

    Crash SOS & Location Alert

    When a high-G collision is detected, SafeRide initiates a 10-second cancel countdown. If uncancelled, an SOS SMS with live GPS Google Maps coordinates is dispatched to emergency contacts.

    • MPU-6050 3-axis accelerometer & gyroscope tracks sudden deceleration shock & tilt.
    • 10-second loud piezo buzzer countdown allows unhurt riders to abort false alarms.
    • Automated SMS with Google Maps pin sent via GSM or paired smartphone bridge.
    Hardware: MPU-6050 Accelerometer + GPS NEO-6M / GSM SIM800L or BLE Bridge
    Important Safety Standard Note: SafeRide does not replace a certified helmet. It is an auxiliary smart safety module created to retrofit existing BIS/ISI, DOT, or ECE certified helmets without compromising energy-absorbing EPS foam linings.
    Intelligent Workflow

    How It Works

    From the moment you strap your helmet to emergency communication after a collision, the system operates with automated fail-safes designed around rider habits.

    1

    Wear & Buckle

    Put on your certified helmet and fasten the chin strap. The tactile switch senses strap closure and wakes the ESP32 microcontroller from sleep.

    CIRCUIT CLOSED · ARMED
    2

    Ride Safely

    The module monitors riding telemetry in the background. Digital filtering algorithms prevent normal road vibrations or potholes from triggering false alarms.

    SAMPLING AT 50Hz · STABLE
    3

    Crash Detected

    If severe deceleration (>4.5G) occurs, the 85dB piezo buzzer pulses and starts a 10-second cancel window so unhurt riders can dismiss false alarms.

    10s GRACE CANCEL WINDOW
    4

    SOS Sent

    If the 10 seconds elapse without cancellation, an automated SMS containing precise latitude, longitude, and a Google Maps link is broadcast to emergency contacts.

    GPS COORDINATES BROADCAST
    * Disclaimer: Crash detection is an assistance feature and may not detect every crash. Mobile network coverage and battery status are required for SOS dispatch.

    Comprehensive Protection

    Built-in Intelligence for Everyday Commutes

    Six purpose-built safety features designed specifically for the chaotic traffic conditions, variable road surfaces, and high temperatures of Indian riding.

    Strap-Check Alert

    Continuous physical interlock sensing verifies that the chin strap buckle remains firmly fastened throughout the commute, providing audio-visual nudges if left unbuckled.

    Crash Detection

    6-axis motion analysis (3-axis accelerometer + 3-axis gyro) identifies high-deceleration impact spikes and abnormal head rollover angles exceeding normal motorcycle maneuvers.

    10-Second Cancel Window

    A deliberate audible countdown sounds immediately upon impact. If you merely dropped your helmet while stationary or are unharmed, a quick push button aborts the SMS.

    Emergency-Contact SOS

    Direct SMS broadcast to pre-configured family contacts and local emergency services (112), bypassing the need to unlock or reach for your phone during an injury.

    GPS Location Sharing

    Transmits exact latitude and longitude pin links directly to Google Maps, shaving critical minutes off ambulance response and search times on rural highways.

    Low-Power Sleep Mode

    When the helmet is taken off and unbuckled, the ESP32 automatically transitions into deep sleep mode, conserving battery power for up to 30+ hours of ride time.

    Universal Retrofit Compatibility: SafeRide clips securely to standard micrometric quick-release straps as well as double-D ring harnesses. It does not require modifying, drilling, or replacing your certified helmet shell.

    An open engineering approach: testing real hardware, measuring limitations, and optimizing the Bill of Materials to keep the module affordable for every two-wheeler owner.

    Real-World Testing & Redesign Insights

    During road trials on campus test tracks and urban roads, we logged specific failure points that guided our firmware revisions:

    ISSUE 1

    Speed Breaker False Alerts

    Sharp rumble strips and abrupt speed bumps initially produced acceleration spikes that resembled low-speed falls.

    → Solution: Raised detection threshold to >4.5G and added gyro rotational velocity cross-checking.

    ISSUE 2

    Accidental Drops During Parking

    Dropping a helmet on the ground while taking it off registered high Gs without a rider inside.

    → Solution: 10-second loud buzzer cancel window gives the rider immediate opportunity to dismiss.

    ISSUE 3

    Breadboard Bulk & Battery Drain

    Initial Arduino UNO test rig was heavy (210g) and drained a 9V cell in less than 4 hours.

    → Solution: Migrated to ESP32 with Li-Po pouch cell + deep-sleep states, cutting weight to under 80g.
